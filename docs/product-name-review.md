# Provisional product name and conflict review

Checked: **2026-09-13 UTC**. Decision: use **Read to Lead** for the local prototype
as requested by the owner; **hold final brand adoption** pending qualified clearance
or a new name. **Read 2 Lead** and **Read2Lead** are not cleared alternatives.
This is a bounded public-source screen, not a legal opinion or an availability
guarantee. A possible US-first release is the search assumption; markets remain open.

[Companies House](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/11429999/filing-history)
records CLAY's company-name change to Read to Lead Holdings Limited on February 6,
2026; the inspected US trademark record still shows CLAY. This is a follow-up lead,
not an independently verified assignment chain.

USPTO explains that confusing similarity may involve sound, meaning or commercial
impression, and that common-law rights can arise through use. Accordingly, changing
“to” to “2,” adding “Junior/Kids,” or using “Read to Lead: Robot Workshop” does not
by itself resolve this conflict screen. That is a risk assessment, not a court
finding. See [likelihood of confusion](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion)
and [comprehensive clearance scope](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks).

## Decision required before final identity

The screen covered exact/close phrases, public products and three specific US
records. It did **not** complete a full similarity search across classes 9/41/42,
state registers, all common-law uses, international registries, litigation or
priority/ownership histories. Domain and social-handle availability were not checked.

The owner should select a more distinctive shortlist or obtain a qualified US
trademark assessment of these names, precise app/services descriptions and intended
markets. **Little Word Leaders**, **Readtrail Workshop** and **Letterlight Leaders**
are brainstorming directions only; they have not been screened or recommended as
available. Record the selected name and clearance outcome before final brand
production, permanent technical IDs, repository migration or store submission.
No attorney was contacted, mark filed, domain purchased or store name reserved here.
