## Commission in stages

These allowances describe proposed scope, not vendor estimates, budgets, promised
turnaround or approved spending. Request fixed-fee or capped quotes for equivalent
deliverables from two suitable candidates per needed role. Stop sourcing when the
comparison supports a decision; a permanent team is unnecessary for M1.

| 0 — local preparation | Publication safeguards, exact review inputs and generic briefs; owner identifies initial markets and future confidential IP | Private procurement location ready. Repository visibility is a separate owner decision. |

Expand coverage before broader device claims. |
| 5 — authorized feasibility pilot | Human-led parent permission/child assent and minimal private observations after child-use gates pass | Help, instruction, frustration and finishing findings; continue/revise/stop decision. This is not an efficacy study. |

Avoid a full voice library before script approval, final branding before clearance,
or a broad device sweep against a changing prototype. Compare total accepted-output
cost: quote, rights, editing, pickups, retest, platform fees and tax. Keep actual
prices and contingency private. A dependable total needs equivalent quotes and
hardware coverage; a proposed paid sample is not an authorized purchase.

## Contractor brief and selection

Use this template privately when outreach is authorized. Share only role-relevant
inputs with suitable confidentiality/rights terms. A public repository link
discloses its contents; an NDA does not undo prior publication.

```text
Assignment: [literacy / voice / counsel / accessibility / device QA]
Outcome: approve or identify blockers in two offline English lessons.
Audience: beginning readers, approximately ages 4–7, US English models.
Product: hear/read, assemble, test, celebrate, finish in a robot workshop.
Scope: two lessons; no accounts, microphone, live AI, ads or analytics.
Inputs: exact source/content version and role-specific materials below.
Deliverables: findings, corrections, final files if applicable, and one retest.
Evidence: reviewer identifier, date, exact version/files, scope and limitations.
Quote: capped/fixed fee, inclusions, pickups, rights, fees/tax and change rate.
Schedule: earliest start, turnaround and dependencies.
Experience: relevant examples, credentials and references we may verify.
Privacy: synthetic data only; no child recordings; private retention/deletion.
Rights: explicit distribution/editing scope; disclose third-party materials.
Decision: owner acceptance/payment is separate from specialist approval.
```

Compare role fit, sample/report quality, rights clarity, scope, retest terms, total
cost and availability privately. Reject unverifiable credentials, missing grants,
guaranteed legal/store approval or shared-signing requests. Verify a lawyer through
the relevant licensing authority. Do not select by hourly rate alone.

## Review packet and acceptance

[Content review](content-review.md) remains the actual approval procedure. No new
catalog schema or alternate approval system is introduced.

| Role | Inputs | Required output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Literacy | `content/catalog.json`, `content/audio-scripts.json`, runtime instructions, adult walkthrough and existing review questions | Step-level corrections, introduced/assumed patterns, oral directions, connected-text review and sound guidance; final listening sign-off tied to exact assets/pack version |
| Voice/producer | Literacy-approved script, stable clip IDs, pronunciation notes, reference delivery and rights scope | Clean human phonemes, no added schwa/letter-name substitution, consistent tone/level, no clipping or clipped consonants; editable masters/runtime files; creator attribution/private grant reference |
| Rights/counsel | Asset inventory/licenses, contracts, data map, runtime/SDK evidence, markets, publisher and claims | Scope-specific decision/open issues; agreed distribution, editing, territories, term and promotional rights. Any synthetic voice/training rights need separate consideration, not silent inclusion. |
| QA/accessibility | Approved build/hash, source revision, content digest, device matrix and existing tests | Device/OS/build-specific results, reproducible failures/retest, native assistive/offline/traffic/backup evidence, exclusions and risks |

Listen to every final clip after editing/export, then in context on tablet speakers
and headphones. Keep effects quiet during sound models. Agree master/runtime formats
and level targets with the producer and verify native playback compatibility;
do not invent an industry-wide loudness standard for phonics.

Public-safe asset references can contain creator, source/license or private grant
ID, attribution, version/hash and review state. Contracts, legal advice, auditions
and private reviewer details stay outside tracked files. The digest proves
consistency; it does not authenticate a reviewer or confer rights.
