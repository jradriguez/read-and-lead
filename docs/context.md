# Read and Lead

Read and Lead is an English reading adventure for beginning readers. These terms
keep instruction, game progress, and evidence of learning distinct.

## Language

**Learner**:
The child practicing reading, independently or with a parent.
_Avoid_: Customer, account owner

**Guided independence**:
Practice that a child can navigate through spoken guidance and demonstrations,
with optional parent participation.
_Avoid_: Unsupervised tutoring, replacement teacher

**Mission**:
A small invention or discovery goal that gives the reading activities a purpose.
_Avoid_: Test, assessment

**Lesson**:
A planned sequence that introduces or practices specified reading skills.
_Avoid_: Level when referring to instruction

**Sound-spelling pattern**:
A relationship between a speech sound and the letter or letters representing it
in a word.
_Avoid_: Letter name when referring to a speech sound

**Independent response**:
A correct first response to an activity without hints or a demonstrated answer.
_Avoid_: Proven mastery, reading level

**Assisted response**:
A correct response following a hint, model, or correction within the activity.
_Avoid_: Independent success

**Completion**:
Finishing a lesson, including with support. It records participation and game
progress without establishing that the learner can apply every skill independently.
_Avoid_: Mastery, proficiency certification

**Transfer**:
Applying a taught reading skill to a word or sentence not practiced in the same
activity.
_Avoid_: Memorization, reward count
